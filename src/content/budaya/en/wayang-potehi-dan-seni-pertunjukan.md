If one thing sets the Lasem stage apart, it is the meeting of Chinese and Javanese traditions. The two do not merely sit side by side; they share a stage, an audience, and a repertoire. In this small town the performing arts grew as a conversation, not a dividing wall.

## Wayang potehi

Wayang potehi is glove puppetry of Chinese origin, also known as *bu dai xi*. The puppeteer slips a hand into each puppet from behind a small wooden stage, telling classic Chinese tales to the sound of a band. In Lasem the art nearly disappeared, then was revived by residents and local troupes. It is the clearest example of how a tradition survives precisely because people care for it together.

## Wayang krucil and wayang bengkong

Lasem also knows **Wayang Krucil**, small flat puppets that carry Panji and chronicle tales, and **Wayang Bengkong**, another coastal variant. Together they show that puppetry in Lasem was never single, but many-branched.

## Laesan and barongan

**Laesan** is a trance dance particular to Lasem, in which performers fall into trance and dance to the rhythm. Alongside it are the **Barongsai and Liong**, the Chinese lion and dragon dances, as well as **Jaran Kepang and Barongan**, **Tayub**, **Ketoprak**, and the **Arak-arakan Ancak** procession. Among them stands **Thong-thong Klek**, a bamboo percussion ensemble whose sound is a signature of Lasem.

## Patol Sarang and the patholan dance

In Sarang district there is **Patol Sarang**, a traditional wrestling art performed during Sedekah Laut. From it came **Tari Patholan**, a modern dance created in 2017 by Puji Purwati of Sanggar Galuh Ajeng, inspired by Patol and staged for the regency's anniversary.

> On a Lasem stage, Javanese gamelan and Chinese music can share the same evening. What joins them is a single audience.

## Gending Lasem

In Javanese gamelan, the name Lasem also lives in the *gending* "Pathet Lasem" and "Sampak Lasem". Both show that this town is not only where art is performed, but a place that helped name the Javanese repertoire itself.
