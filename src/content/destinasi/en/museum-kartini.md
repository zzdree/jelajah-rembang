The R.A. Kartini Museum stands at Jl. Gatot Subroto No. 8 in Kutoharjo village, only about 300 metres from the centre of Rembang city. The building was once the official residence of the Regent of Rembang, where R.A. Kartini lived after her marriage to R.M.A.A. Djoyodiningrat. The museum began in a single room and was extended in 2011 to cover the whole house.

## What is on display

The collection centres on Kartini's life and thinking: her letters, personal belongings, photographs from childhood to adulthood, and a large portrait. Some items relate to her brother, RMP Sosrokartono, along with antiquities found in and around Rembang. The rooms give a sense of the world of a late nineteenth-century Javanese aristocratic family.

## The woman behind it

R.A. Kartini was born in 1879 and died in 1904, still young. Her letters on women's education and freedom of thought became a foundation of Indonesia's emancipation movement, and her birthday, 21 April, is observed each year as Kartini Day.

## Getting there

The museum is easy to reach because it sits inside the city. From the Rembang town square, head for Jl. Gatot Subroto, or simply walk if you are staying in the centre.
