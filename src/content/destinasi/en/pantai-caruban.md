Caruban Beach lies in Gedongmulyo village, Lasem district, about 12 kilometres from the Rembang town square. Its sand is fine and pale, unlike the darker sand of many beaches on Java's north coast. The site spreads over roughly 21 hectares, with a line of casuarina trees along the shore.

## Sunsets and a historical trace

Caruban is busiest as the sun goes down, when the sky over the Java Sea shifts colour and the shoreline turns into a quiet backdrop. Not far away are the remains of the Taman Sitaresmi wall at Caruban Gedongmulyo, a fragment of Lasem's past. In the distance visitors can make out Gunung Kajar and the towers of the power plant at Sluke, while fishing boats complete the coastal scene.

## What you can do

Watching the sunset, walking the shoreline, and taking photographs are the main activities here. The sea is fairly clear, with a blue-green tint. Several stalls around the beach sell seafood, a good stop for a late-afternoon meal before heading back to the city.

## Getting there

From the Lasem main road the beach is about 2 kilometres away, west of SMPN 1 Lasem, and it appears on digital maps. From the Rembang town square the drive takes about 20 minutes by private vehicle.
