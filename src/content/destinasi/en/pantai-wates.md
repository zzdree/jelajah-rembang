Wates Beach, also known as Wates White-Sand Beach (*Pasir Putih Wates*), is one of the newer coastal destinations opened to visitors in Rembang Regency. It sits in Pancur district, on the western side of the regency. Because it is less developed and less crowded than other beaches, the atmosphere feels calmer and simpler.

## A quieter shore

The sand is pale and the water clear, and the waves are generally small. For anyone who wants to unwind away from the crowds, this beach is a sensible choice. Trees along the edge offer shade, and the open sea view feels roomy.

## What you can do

Walking the shoreline, swimming in the safe sections, and sitting to take in the view are the usual activities. Facilities here are not as complete as at the larger beaches, so it is best to bring your own supplies. Morning or late afternoon is the best time to visit, when the sun is gentler.

## Getting there

From Rembang city, drive west towards Pancur district. It is worth asking local residents for the current route, since the road to the beach can change with the season. The trip is most comfortable by private vehicle.
