The grave of R.A. Kartini lies in Bulu village, south of Rembang city. Kartini died in 1904 and was buried here with her family. The burial ground is one of the city's landmark sites and has been reworked several times to make it tidier and more comfortable for visitors.

## A place of pilgrimage

Besides Kartini's grave, the same site holds the grave of her husband, R.M.A.A. Djoyodiningrat, the grave of their only son, and the graves of the Rembang regents' family from that period. The area works as a single pilgrimage ground rather than one focal point.

Every 21 April, on Kartini Day, the site draws many visitors. On other days it is quieter, well suited to a calm visit.

## Around the grave

After paying respects, visitors often stop at the stalls nearby for local food and souvenirs. The grounds are shaded and orderly.

## Getting there

From Rembang city, head south towards Bulu village, about 17.5 kilometres away. The site is well placed and reachable by public or private transport.
