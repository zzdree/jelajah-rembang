Lodan Reservoir (*Embung Lodan*) is a small reservoir in Sarang district, the easternmost part of Rembang Regency, close to the border with East Java. An *embung*, or small reservoir, collects rainwater and surface runoff, which is then used to irrigate fields and gardens in the dry season. Its value to farming comes well before any value to tourism.

## The surrounding view

Sarang district is known for its limestone hills, which set it apart from the coastal plain to the west. Around the reservoir, a calm sheet of water reflects the sky, ringed by hills that are bare or covered in scrub. The mood is quiet and plain, far from the bustle of developed resorts.

## What you can do

There are few facilities here. The sensible things are to sit and take in the view, take photographs, or simply pause if you happen to pass by. As this is not a specially managed tourist area, visitors should come self-sufficient and leave the place clean.

## Getting there

From Rembang city, head east towards Sarang district, at the far end of the regency. It is best to ask local residents about the road to the reservoir, since the route is not always obvious to newcomers. A private vehicle is the most comfortable option.
