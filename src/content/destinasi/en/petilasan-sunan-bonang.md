The Sunan Bonang complex stands in Bonang village, Lasem district, about 4 kilometres north of Lasem town. This is where Sunan Bonang, one of the nine saints (*Wali Songo*) who spread Islam across Java, spent the last part of his life. The site is among the most important religious destinations in Rembang.

## One complex, several places

The complex gathers several points into a single area. There is a mosque, then the **pasujudan**, a flat andesite stone inside a small shelter on a hill, believed to be where Sunan Bonang prostrated himself, facing the Java Sea directly. Nearby is the grave of the Champa Princess (*Putri Campa*), a figure linked to the spread of Islam in this region. Roughly a kilometre away lies the **ndalem**, Sunan Bonang's former residence, now a burial ground that includes his unmarked tomb.

## The Bende Becak and an annual rite

Among the historic objects kept here is the **Bende Becak**, a gong linked to Sunan Bonang's travels. Each year the **Penjamasan Bende Becak** ceremony is held, in which the gong is cleansed and cared for in a procession. The rite draws pilgrims and residents from across the region.

## Getting there

From Lasem town, head north about 4 kilometres to Bonang village. From Rembang city the distance is about 17 kilometres east, towards Surabaya. Reaching the pasujudan involves climbing a flight of steps, as it sits on a hill.
